Item Retention Policy

Storage Duration

Found items are held in the Main Office (Room 2207) for 30 days from the date they are turned in. This timeframe allows students adequate opportunity to search for and claim their belongings.

High-Value Items

Electronics, jewelry, wallets, and other high-value items may be held for an extended period of up to 60 days at the discretion of administration. Students will be notified if their item qualifies for extended storage.

Post-Retention Period

After the retention period expires, unclaimed items will be:

  • Donated to local charities and community organizations
  • Disposed of according to school district waste management policies
  • Returned to the original finder if they request it within 5 days of expiration

Claim Verification Policy

Required Information

To verify ownership and process claims fairly, students must provide:

  • Valid CPS school email address
  • Current student ID number
  • Approximate date the item was lost
  • Specific identifying features (brand, color, unique marks, contents, etc.)

Verification Process

All claims are reviewed by administrative staff who:

  • Compare submitted details with the actual found item
  • Verify student identity through the CPS system
  • May request additional proof of ownership for high-value items
  • Approve or deny claims within 1-3 school days

Multiple Claims

If multiple students claim the same item, the claim with the most accurate and detailed description will be prioritized. In cases where verification is inconclusive, students may be asked to provide photo evidence or purchase receipts.

False Claims

Submitting false or fraudulent claims is a violation of school policy. Students found attempting to claim items that do not belong to them may face disciplinary action and suspension of Lost & Found privileges.

Prohibited Items

The following items cannot be accepted into the Lost & Found system and should be reported directly to school administration or security:

Safety Concerns

  • Weapons or weapon-like objects
  • Medications (prescription or over-the-counter)
  • Lighters or matches
  • Chemicals or hazardous materials

Contraband

  • Tobacco products or vaping devices
  • Alcohol or illegal substances
  • Stolen property
  • Items violating school code of conduct

Other Exclusions

  • Live animals or plants
  • Opened food or beverages
  • Items with biohazard concerns
  • Inappropriate or explicit materials

Note: School ID cards, library cards, and official school documents should be turned in directly to the Main Office rather than submitted through the online system.

Liability Disclaimer

Walnut Hills High School and Cincinnati Public Schools are not responsible for:

  • Lost, stolen, or damaged personal property on school premises
  • Items that are not turned in to the Lost & Found
  • Delays in processing claims or item retrieval
  • Items damaged while in Lost & Found storage
  • Incorrect disposal of items after the retention period

Students bring personal belongings to school at their own risk. We strongly encourage students to:

  • Label valuable items with their name and student ID
  • Keep track of their belongings throughout the school day
  • Report lost items as soon as possible
  • Check the Lost & Found regularly

Insurance: Students with valuable items (laptops, instruments, etc.) should consider personal property insurance. The school's insurance does not cover student personal belongings.

Credits & Rewards Policy

Earning Credits

Students earn 10 credits when an item they turned in is successfully claimed by its rightful owner. Credits are automatically added to the student's account once the claim is approved and the item is picked up.

Credit Restrictions

  • Credits cannot be transferred between students
  • Credits expire at the end of each school year
  • No credits are awarded for items turned in but never claimed
  • Credits may be revoked if fraudulent activity is detected

Redeeming Rewards

Credits can be redeemed for various school rewards through the user homepage. Available rewards and their credit costs are subject to change based on availability. Current reward options include:

  • Candy and snacks from the Main Office
  • School event tickets and activity passes
  • Gift cards to local businesses
  • School spirit merchandise

Reward Fulfillment

Reward redemption requests are processed within 3-5 school days. Students will be notified via email when their reward is ready for pickup at the Main Office.

Privacy Policy

Information We Collect

The WHHS Lost & Found system collects and stores the following information:

  • Student Information: CPS email address, student ID number, name
  • Item Information: Descriptions, photos, locations, dates
  • Claim Information: Verification details, claim status, timestamps
  • Account Activity: Credits earned, rewards redeemed, submission history

How We Use Your Information

Collected information is used exclusively for:

  • Matching lost items with their rightful owners
  • Verifying student identity and claim legitimacy
  • Awarding credits and processing reward redemptions
  • Communicating with students about claims and items
  • Improving the Lost & Found system functionality
  • Generating anonymous usage statistics

Information Access & Security

Who has access: Only authorized WHHS administrators and designated Lost & Found staff can access student information. Teacher and non-administrative staff do not have access to the system database.

Security measures: All data is stored on secure CPS servers with encrypted connections. Student passwords are hashed and cannot be viewed by administrators.

Data Retention

Student data is retained according to the following schedule:

  • Active items: Stored until claimed or retention period expires
  • Claimed items: Record kept for current school year plus 1 year
  • Student accounts: Active while enrolled; archived after graduation
  • Contact messages: Kept for 2 years for record-keeping purposes

FERPA Compliance

This system complies with the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA). Student information is considered part of educational records and is protected accordingly. Information will not be shared with third parties without proper consent, except as required by law.

Student Rights

Students have the right to:

  • Request to view all information stored about them
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of their account (contact administration)
  • Opt-out of the rewards system while still using the Lost & Found

Photo & Image Policy

Photos uploaded to the system become part of the Lost & Found records. By uploading an image, students confirm they have the right to share that image and that it does not contain inappropriate content. Images may be removed at administrator discretion.

Contact for Privacy Concerns

Questions or concerns about privacy and data handling should be directed to:
