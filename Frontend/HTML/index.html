How it Works
Through this website, the WHHS Lost and Found system is now digitized! Students can browse for their own lost items as well as report items they may have found or lost themselves.

Claiming Items
To claim an item, students must first visit the Found Items page to submit a request for the item. They will then be asked for their name and student ID as well as some verification questions (unique marks, approximate date lost, etc) to ensure the item is returned to its rightful owner. Upon submitting, your claim will be set as 'Pending' and given to an administrator to approve or deny.

Pickup Information
When one of your claims is approved, visit the main office, 2207, to pick up your item. The office is open Monday - Friday from 7:30 AM to 3:15 PM.

Unclaimed Property
To avoid an abundance of lost items, property that has been left in the lost and found for more than 60 days will be donated.

Reward System
When someone claims a lost item that you submitted to the website, you will receive 10 credits. Credits function like WHHS Eagle Bucks, which you can spend in the lost and found shop for things like candy, gift cards, and tickets to school events.
