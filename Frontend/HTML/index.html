Walnut's Official Lost and Found

HOW IT WORKS

Through this website, the WHHS Lost and Found system is now digitized! Students can browse for their own lost items as well as report items they may have found or lost themselves

CLAIMING ITEMS

To claim an item, students submit a request on the Found Items page with their name, student ID, and verification details. The claim is then sent to an administrator for approval or denial.

PICKUP INFORMATION

All items should be picked up and dropped off at the 2207 Main Office.

REWARDS SYSTEM

Turn in items! After that item is claimed by another student, you earn credits and can then redeem school rewards.

QUESTIONS?

Scroll down to our Frequently Asked Questions section or go visit the Contact page to get in touch with an admin.

FREQUENTLY ASKED Q'S

Found items are typically held in the Main Office (2207) for 30 days. After this period, unclaimed items may be donated to local charities or disposed of according to school policy. High-value items like phones or jewelry may be held longer.

When submitting a claim, you'll need your CPS email, student ID, and specific verification details about the item (such as unique features, what's inside a bag or wallet, exact color/brand, or when and where you lost it). The more specific details you provide, the faster your claim can be approved.

Yes! Anyone can browse the Found Items page to search for their belongings. However, you must be logged in with your CPS school email to submit a claim request or report found/lost items.

Credits earned from turning in found items can be redeemed for various school rewards such as free candy, school event tickets, and gift cards. Visit your user homepage to view available rewards and your current credit balance.

If you believe an item was incorrectly claimed by another student, contact an administrator immediately through our Contact page with your student ID and details about the item. All claims are reviewed and approved by administrators, and disputes will be investigated to ensure items reach their rightful owners.
